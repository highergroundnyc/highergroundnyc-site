ABOUT

A tattoo studio in the heart of Midtown Manhattan.

Higher Ground NYC is a professional tattoo studio welcoming both New Yorkers and visitors from around the world.

Just minutes from Times Square, Bryant Park, and Penn Station, our studio provides a clean, comfortable, and creative environment where clients work directly with experienced tattoo artists to bring their ideas to life.

Artists & Styles

Our team of tattoo artists works across a variety of styles.

Whether you're looking for a small tattoo to remember your time in New York, your first tattoo, or a larger custom piece, we'll help connect you with an artist whose work fits your idea and style.

Every tattoo begins with understanding what you want — the concept, placement, size, references, and the story behind it — so we can create a piece that feels personal to you.

Custom Tattoos. Personal Experience.

At Higher Ground NYC, we believe a great tattoo experience is about more than the final artwork.

From your first consultation through the tattoo session and aftercare, our goal is to make the process clear, comfortable, professional, and personal.

We welcome appointments, consultations, and walk-in inquiries, depending on artist availability. Whether you live in New York or you're visiting the city, you're always welcome at Higher Ground NYC.
